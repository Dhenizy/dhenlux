What it does

📋 Order ticketsAdd items from your own price list and get a ticket number for every order.
🔄 Automatic statusOrders turn Ready at the pickup time you set. Tap Collected when the customer pays.
💬 WhatsApp alertsOne tap sends a message when clothes are ready, with your bank details if there is a balance.
💰 Money owedSee part-payments and unpaid balances at a glance.
⏳ Date changesMove a pickup date, record the reason and apologise to the customer in one tap.
📅 Calendar remindersAdd pickups to Google Calendar so you are reminded before the date.

How it works

  1. Set your prices and bank details once.
  2. Add each order when a customer drops off clothes.
  3. Set the pickup time, then send the WhatsApp message when the order turns Ready.
Good to knowYour orders are saved on the phone or computer you use. Use the “Download all orders” button in the app regularly as a backup.

📅 Pickup reminders
In Google Calendar, create a calendar called “Dry Cleaning Pickups”. In its settings, under “All-day event notifications”, add 3 days, 2 days and 1 day before (choose notification, email, or both). Then tap “Add to calendar” on any order and pick that calendar.
💾 Backup your orders
CSV is a simple spreadsheet file. This button saves all your orders into one, and you can open it in Excel or Google Sheets. Download it often, because if your phone or browser data is cleared, the orders in this app are lost.
